### About Me

<div class="large-margin" style="text-align:center">
	<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/pictures/Doris_Valpo_Mural.jpg" width="75%" alt="Doris Stoppacher"/>
</div>
I am a computational astrophysicist (PhD from UAM, Dec. 2019) specialising in galaxy formation and evolution, with a strong focus on methodological innovation, open science, and the development of reproducible tools for the astronomical community.

My research combines cosmological simulations, machine learning, and data-intensive analyses to investigate how galaxies form and evolve across the full mass and surface-brightness spectrum. I aim at combining “The Extremes” populations of galaxy formation – from diffuse <i>low-surface-brightness galaxies (LSBGs)</i> and <i>bright cluster galaxies (BCGs)</i>. These systems occupy opposite extremes of our known galaxy population, yet arise from the same underlying physics, making them uniquely valuable for improving our understanding of how galaxies and their underlying structure form and evolve in the Universe.

My scientific path has taken me across several countries and research cultures. After five years in Madrid, I spent one year at the University of California, Santa Cruz and two years at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile in Santiago. In 2024, I joined the computational astrophysics group at the Universidad de Sevilla. I am actively creating research networks and fostering international collaboration with collaborators in for example, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, USA, Spain, UK, and China. 

Alongside my research, I am committed to teaching, mentoring, outreach, and inclusive scientific practice. I am a member of <a href="https://astronomersforplanet.earth/" target="_blank"><i>Astronomers for Planet Earth (A4E)</i></a> and the <a href="https://www.sea-astronomia.es/" target="_blank"><i>Sociedad Española de Astronomía (SEA)</i></a>, and an advocate for student mental health, cultural equity, and inclusion in astrophysics. I regularly give talks in schools and serve as a “women in science” representative, promoting equity, visibility, and inclusion in STEM.

In recognition of my scientific work and community engagement, I was named <a href="https://oepg.at/de/news/2025-05-01-physikerin-des-monats-doris-stoppacher" target="_blank"><i>Physicist of the Month</i></a> by the Austrian Physical Society in 2025.
