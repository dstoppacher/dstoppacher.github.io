### About Me

<div class="large-margin" style="text-align:center">
	<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/pictures/Doris_Valpo_Mural.jpg" width="75%" alt="Doris Stoppacher"/>
</div>
I am a computational astrophysicist (PhD from UAM, Dec. 2019) specialising in galaxy formation and evolution, with a strong focus on methodological innovation, open science, and the development of reproducible tools for the astronomical community.

My research combines cosmological simulations, machine learning, and data-intensive analyses to investigate how galaxies form and evolve across the full mass and surface-brightness spectrum – from diffuse <i>low-surface-brightness galaxies (LSBGs)</i> and bright cluster galaxies (BCGs). I have built an independent, internationally recognised research line centred on “The Extremes” populations of galaxy formation, integrating theory, observations, and modern data-driven methods.

Alongside my research, I am committed to teaching, mentoring, outreach, and inclusive scientific practice. I am a member of <a href="https://astronomersforplanet.earth/" target="_blank"><i>Astronomers for Planet Earth (A4E)</i></a> and the <a href="https://www.sea-astronomia.es/" target="_blank"><i>Sociedad Española de Astronomía (SEA)</i></a>, and an advocate for student mental health, cultural equity, and inclusion in astrophysics.
